## Brand Identity

Application Name: **RecoIA**

The entire interface should be branded as **RecoIA**, an AI-powered intelligent recommendation platform.

### Brand Personality

RecoIA is:

* Modern
* Intelligent
* Trustworthy
* Minimalist
* Professional
* Fast
* Data-driven

The branding should communicate that RecoIA is an advanced AI recommendation system, not just an online store.

### Logo

Design a clean and modern logo for **RecoIA**.

Requirements:

* Minimalist
* Flat design
* Easily recognizable
* Looks like a real startup logo
* Include a simple AI-inspired icon (network nodes, abstract neural connections, or a geometric symbol)
* The icon should work well as a favicon.
* Use the primary color with a monochrome version for dark mode.

### Branding

Use **RecoIA** consistently throughout the application.

Examples:

* RecoIA Dashboard
* RecoIA Analytics
* RecoIA Recommendations
* Welcome to RecoIA
* Powered by RecoIA AI
* RecoIA Admin Console

Do not use placeholder names such as "E-Commerce", "Shop", "Dashboard", or "AI Recommendation System". Everything should be fully branded as **RecoIA**.

### Landing Page

The hero section should immediately communicate the product's value.

Headline:

**Discover Products You'll Actually Love with AI**

Subheading:

"RecoIA uses advanced artificial intelligence to understand your preferences and deliver highly personalized product recommendations based on your interests, browsing behavior, and interactions."

Primary CTA:
**Get Started**

Secondary CTA:
**Learn More**

Include a modern illustration or abstract AI-inspired visual that matches the professional branding.

### Browser & Metadata

Set:

* Browser title: **RecoIA – AI-Powered Recommendation Platform**
* Favicon: RecoIA logo
* Loading screen: RecoIA logo with a subtle loading animation
* Footer: **© 2026 RecoIA. All rights reserved.**

The application should feel like a real SaaS product that could be launched commercially, not a student project or an AI-generated demo.
